## What is Flow Builder?

Flow Builder is a Salesforce automation tool used to automate business processes without coding. It helps businesses automate repetitive tasks, send notifications, update records, and improve productivity.

---

## Types of Flows

### Screen Flow
Screen Flow interacts with users through screens and forms. It is mainly used to collect information from users.

### Record Triggered Flow
Record Triggered Flow runs automatically when a record is created, updated, or deleted.

---

## Automation Ideas

1. Auto email after student registration
2. Auto generate student ID
3. Fee reminder notification
4. Auto update available seats
5. Notify faculty when course is full

## Manual vs Automated Process

### Manual Process
Staff manually checks fee records and sends reminders to students.

### Problems
- Time consuming
- Human errors
- Missed reminders
- Delayed communication

### Automated Process
Salesforce automatically checks due dates and sends reminders.

### Benefits
- Faster process
- Better productivity
- Accurate reminders
- Saves employee effort

---

## Reflection

Companies automate repetitive business processes to save time, reduce manual errors, improve consistency, and increase productivity. Automation helps businesses provide faster and better services.
